# OEM ground-clearance research batch — 2024–2025 model years

**Status: CLEARANCE FIELD VERIFIED FOR THE SPECIFIC 2025 SOURCE SCOPE ONLY.** Other catalog fields remain unverified unless separately sourced. This batch does not authorize a loading decision; measure the actual vehicle on scene.

## Ford F-150 — 2025

Ford's 2025 US F-150 specification page lists minimum running ground clearance of 8.2 in on standard configurations, 9.4 in on some Tremor configurations and 12.0 in on Raptor. The database uses 8.2 in as a conservative baseline. Identify the exact trim, tire and suspension package.

Source: https://www.ford.com/trucks/f150/2025/pricing-and-incentives/

## Ford Super Duty — 2025

Ford's 2025 technical specifications list configuration-specific minimum running ground clearance. Lowest listed values are 9.2 in for F-250 SRW, 9.1 in for F-350 SRW and 9.2 in for F-350 DRW. Cab, bed, drivetrain and tire package affect the value. No F-450 dually value was added because the retrieved official table does not provide a matching F-450 DRW row.

Source: https://www.fromtheroad.ford.com/content/dam/fordmediasite/us/en/library/2025/specs/2025-Ford-Super-Duty-Technical-Specs.pdf

## Data handling and limitations

- Only the specific 2024 and 2025 model-year slices listed above receive this clearance evidence. Earlier years remain separate and are not upgraded from newer sources; the 2025 Electrified GV70 remains unknown until a matching source is confirmed.
- Ground-clearance source URL, source year, verified value and variant caveat are stored in clearance-specific fields.
- These entries do not upgrade curb weight, wheelbase, body dimensions or overall row confidence.
- The calculator still returns **NO VERDICT — MEASURE FIRST** until actual on-scene clearance and wheelbase are entered.
- Published ground clearance may be measured at a specific underbody point and vehicle condition; it does not account for aftermarket accessories, damage, load, tire changes or modifications.
