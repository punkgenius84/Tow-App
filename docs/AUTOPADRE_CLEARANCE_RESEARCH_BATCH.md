## Interpretation and limits

AutoPadre is a secondary specification source. These entries are therefore explicitly marked third-party source-verified, not OEM verified. The catalog clearance is a conservative published reference for the matched model-year/variant scope, not a VIN-specific measurement or a loading approval. A trim, tire, suspension, accessory, load, or modification difference can change actual clearance. Operators must measure the lowest point and wheelbase on the actual vehicle before loading.

The catalog's overall confidence remains low/unverified for these rows because this batch verifies ground clearance only; it does not certify the other catalog dimensions or weights.
