This application adapts the source dataset by filtering to overlapping US model years and using make/model identity and body-style information in a normalized offline vehicle catalog. The resulting records are combined with other sources and are not a verbatim copy of the upstream CSV files. Changes include filtering, normalization, model matching, and integration into the Tow Guide catalog.

No endorsement by the source creator is implied.

**Important scope limit:** This attribution applies only to the US model/year/body-style identity data from this repository. It does not grant rights to, or resolve the redistribution status of, the separate AutoEvolution-derived vehicle specification data described in [docs/DATA_LICENSE_REMEDIATION.md](docs/DATA_LICENSE_REMEDIATION.md). That source remains on hold pending permission or replacement.
